American Literature: Novels of the 1920s
Team A: This novelist wrote about Jay Gatsby and the green light across the
bay in a 1925 novel set on Long Island.
                                ANSWER: F. Scott Fitzgerald [or Francis Scott Key Fitzgerald]
Team B: This 1926 novel follows Jake Barnes to the running of the bulls.
                                ANSWER: The Sun Also Rises
Tossup: This author of "Babbitt" became the first American to win the Nobel
Prize in Literature.
                                ANSWER: Sinclair Lewis [or Harry Sinclair Lewis]

three plus four times five minus two.
