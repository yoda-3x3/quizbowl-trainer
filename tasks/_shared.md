### Answer checking is solved — do not reimplement it

`qb-answer-checker` is installed and wrapped by `judge()`. Measured behaviour:

- Strictness runs **backwards**: 1 is most lenient, 12 strictest, and 3-9 are an
  identical plateau. Default is 7.
- Typo tolerance depends on answerline markup, not strictness. Prefer the HTML
  `answer` field over `answerSanitized` when calling `judge()`.
- Diacritics are already folded. "Salvador Dali" accepts for "Salvador Dalí".
- Matching is token-containment based, so "not michelangelo" is accepted for
  "Michelangelo". This is known and deliberately unguarded.
